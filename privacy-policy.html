9 Miles Media Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 9 Miles Media ("9 Miles Media," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website (the "Site") and our use of Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) to manage and publish content to our own social media accounts.

1. Who We Are

9 Miles Media is a media and content business. This policy covers the Site on which it is published, along with the following social media accounts, which we own and administer:

  • Facebook Page: facebook.com/9milesmedia
  • Instagram: @milesmedia9

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide to us. If you contact us through the Site, by email, or through a social media message, we collect whatever information you choose to share (such as your name, email address, and the content of your message) in order to respond to you.
  • Automatically collected information. Like most websites, the Site may use cookies or similar technologies, and our hosting and analytics providers may automatically collect standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, to help us understand how the Site is used and to keep it secure.
  • Information from our own social media accounts. We use Meta's Graph API to manage and publish content to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account listed above, and to view engagement metrics (such as likes, comments, reach, and impressions) on our own posts. This access is limited to our own Page and Instagram account — we do not use this integration to collect personal information about the people who view, like, comment on, or follow our posts. Any interaction you have with our Page or posts (such as a comment) is handled under Meta's own privacy policy in addition to this one.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site;
  • Respond to inquiries and messages sent to us;
  • Publish and manage content on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and understand how that content performs; and
  • Keep the Site and our accounts secure and address technical issues.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site or our social media presence (for example, hosting providers, analytics tools, or workflow-automation tools we use to schedule and publish our own posts);
  • Meta Platforms, Inc., to the extent necessary to publish and manage content on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account, subject to Meta's Privacy Policy; and
  • Legal or safety purposes, if required by law or necessary to protect our rights, users, or the public.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Messages sent to us are kept only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your inquiry.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information you've provided to us. To make such a request, or with any other privacy question, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
