Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Who we are

9 Miles Media provides web design, web development, and social media / digital marketing services. This policy explains how we handle data in connection with our use of LinkedIn's API — specifically, the LinkedIn Developer application(s) we use to publish marketing content to our own LinkedIn presence, and our 9 Miles Media LinkedIn Company Page.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • The LinkedIn app(s) we operate to automate publishing content to a LinkedIn member profile and/or the 9 Miles Media Company Page on our own behalf.
  • The 9 Miles Media LinkedIn Company Page.

These apps are internal tools we built and operate ourselves. We do not offer them to the public, and no third party authorizes or connects their own LinkedIn account through them.

What data we access

When we authorize our LinkedIn app via OAuth, LinkedIn grants it only the specific permissions we've requested and LinkedIn has approved. Depending on the app, this may include:

  • Basic profile information (name, profile identifier) needed to authenticate the connection.
  • Permission to create posts on our behalf — either to an individual member profile we control, or to the 9 Miles Media Company Page.
  • Basic public engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) on posts we've published, so we can review how our own content performed.

We do not request or access private messages, connection lists, or any other member's personal data through this integration.

How we use this data

We use LinkedIn's API solely to publish scheduled marketing content — text and images — to our own LinkedIn profile and/or Company Page, as part of an internal content-automation workflow. Post content is generated ahead of time by our own tools and reviewed as part of our normal process before it's ever published. Access tokens are used only to authenticate these posting actions.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share any LinkedIn data with third parties.
  • We do not use LinkedIn data for advertising targeting outside of LinkedIn.
  • We do not access or store data belonging to other LinkedIn members beyond the public engagement metrics described above.

Third-party services involved

Some content we publish is assisted by third-party tools — for example, AI writing assistance to draft post copy, and stock-photo services to source images. These tools only ever receive generic topic prompts we provide; they are never given any LinkedIn member data, credentials, or access tokens.

Data storage & security

Access tokens issued to our LinkedIn app are stored in a private, access-controlled automation environment — never exposed publicly. They are retained only for as long as the relevant integration remains active, and are deleted if we disconnect or revoke the app.

Your ability to revoke access

Because these are apps we operate on our own account (not a third-party service others connect to), access can be revoked at any time from LinkedIn's own account settings under Permitted Services. If you have any question about a specific LinkedIn integration tied to 9 Miles Media, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Our LinkedIn presence and related tools are not directed at, and are not knowingly used by, anyone under the age of 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our use of LinkedIn's API changes. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or how 9 Miles Media uses LinkedIn's API can be sent to privacy@9milesmedia.com.
